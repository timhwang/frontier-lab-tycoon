Manual — how this economy works
The bind (both episodes): one fleet, two jobs. Inference serves your shipped model — revenue and growth. Training builds the next one. Every H100e can only do one.

Serving: above 97% utilization you rate-limit: growth penalized, 30% of unmet demand churns to rivals permanently (up to 65% during a launch surge — but a big crowd only drains ~12%/wk, so a large enough storm outlasts the clock no matter what). The collapse clock: Episode 0 dies after 4 consecutive rate-limited weeks (>97%); Episode 1 dies after 6 consecutive weeks of severe under-service (>110%) — mild rate-limiting there is survivable, just costly. Episode 1 demand also saturates toward a ~45T addressable market, so a bigger fleet buys real training headroom.

The frontier: rivals improve every week and ship jumps; 15+ points behind for 6 straight weeks and you are acquired.

Episode 0 (The Trade): 26 weeks, run auto-started, flat +20 on ship, fixed fleet. Ship it, then hold service to the bell.

Episode 1 (The Long Year): 52 weeks; win by surviving to W52 — you will likely need TWO successful ships. Training compute idles until you START a run. The slider is a target: GPUs migrate ~6%/wk, and resizing a live run re-shards it (zero progress that week). The payoff is unknown until final evals: usually +15–24, sometimes a step change, sometimes a dud, rarely (~1 in 12) a total failure. Leases grow the fleet ($60M signing + $2.75M/wk per 5,000, 6-wk lead, max 15,000) — and cash is a real clock: the lab starts unprofitable.

EPISODE 0 — THE TRADE

The core bind, nothing else. One slider: every H100e serving ATLAS-1 customers is one not training ATLAS-2, which pays a guaranteed +20 capability on ship. Fixed fleet, no leases. Ship it and hold service until H1 ends (W26) — through traffic spikes and rival breakthroughs that arrive without warning. Four straight rate-limited weeks is game over.

EPISODE 1 — THE LONG YEAR

A full year: 52 weeks, and one ship probably won't hold the frontier. Training runs start when you say so — and pay out a roulette: step changes, duds, and the occasional total failure. GPUs migrate slowly between jobs; resizing a live run stalls it. Leases can grow the fleet, if the balance sheet survives it. Rivals are faster. Play Episode 0 first.
