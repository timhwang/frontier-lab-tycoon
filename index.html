Manual — how this economy works
Modes (⟲ NEW): Episode 0 is the pure two-way trade — no research, morale, or leases; one slider, a fixed 26-week H1, a guaranteed +20 on ship, and random spikes in both your traffic and rival capability. Its lose rules are tighter: 4 straight rate-limited weeks, or 15+ behind the frontier for 6 weeks. Everything below describes the full Episode 1 economy.

The bind: one fleet, three mouths. Inference earns revenue now; every H100e there is one not training ATLAS-2. Training is your future — rivals improve ~0.7 capability/wk and ship jumps; fall >22 behind for 10 weeks and users evaporate (acquisition, game over). Research looks like waste until you notice it multiplies every training FLOP, cuts divergence risk, and is the main thing keeping researchers from quitting.

Serving: demand > capacity ⇒ rate limits ⇒ growth penalty and 30% of unmet demand churns to rivals permanently. Watch utilization like a hawk near launches.

Morale: researchers want training + research share, comp above market, and a capability lead. Below 45 they start resigning (each senior loss −3% training throughput, forever). Below 25 for 5 weeks = mass exodus.

Win: ship ATLAS-2, then serve the launch: demand jumps at ship and burns hot for ~6 weeks, and you must hold service through the full 8-week launch window — rate-limit 4 of those weeks and the launch flops. (Episode 0 has no flop counter; instead, 4 consecutive rate-limited weeks at any point is game over, and the episode always ends at W26.) During the surge, churn doubles down: 45% of unmet demand leaves for good (vs 30% normally). Plan capacity before you ship; leases take 6 weeks. When the run finishes, its compute frees up — reallocate it immediately. Lose: insolvency, exodus, obsolescence, or a pyrrhic launch.
